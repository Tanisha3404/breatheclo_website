Returns & Exchanges Policy

Overview

Your satisfaction matters. If you are not completely satisfied, you may return or exchange your purchase within 30 days of delivery.

Eligibility

  • Items must be unused, in original packaging, with tags.
  • Includes defective or incorrect items.
  • Not valid for final sale/discounted/customized items.

How to Start a Return/Exchange

  • 1. Visit “My Orders” on your account or contact support.
  • Provide order number and reason.
  • Receive confirmation and return label (domestic shipping may be free, international returns at customer cost).

Process

  • Ship the item via tracked shipping.
  • Once received and inspected, we’ll issue a refund to your original payment method (minus shipping, if applicable).
  • For exchanges, we’ll dispatch the replacement within 3–5 business days after receiving your return.

Refund Timing

Please allow 5–10 business days after we receive the return.

Exceptions & Non‑Returnable Items

  • Personalized/custom items cannot be returned.
  • Final sale items marked at checkout are ineligible.
  • Damaged items must be reported within 48 hours of receipt.
